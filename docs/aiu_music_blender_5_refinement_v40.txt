HOW REFINEMENT WORKS ON THIS PROJECT

Build in small tested steps; every improvement is recorded here, and every recorded improvement names the test that proves it.

The Golden Rule is enforced as a regression: the classic-mode tests (T1 through T4 of the Specification) must stay green after every change, forever.


ITERATION LOG

Iteration 1 (2026-08-07): First working build.

• The analysis pipeline, beat graph, jukebox walk, renderer, mash-up engine, and all three faces were built to Specification version 1, with the test suite of scenarios T1 through T7.

• Decision refined during building: per-beat features average the frames BETWEEN beats (not at the beat instant), because a beat is a span of sound, not a point; this noticeably improves jump seamlessness on synthetic tests.

• Decision refined during building: jump edges exclude the three nearest neighbor beats on each side, because trivially-adjacent jumps are inaudible non-events that crowd out interesting edges.

• Decision refined during building: the walk's end-of-song fallback jumps to the BEST-MATCHING earlier beat rather than beat zero, so the loop point is musical rather than mechanical.

Iteration 9 (2026-08-07): No more cutting mid-vocal-phrase, by owner instruction, after the owner heard jumps chopping words in half.

• Context matching: each pair's similarity distance now blends in the distances of the beats that FOLLOW them (one ahead at weight 0.6, two ahead at weight 0.3), so a jump prefers a landing whose continuation also matches; jumps migrate to phrase boundaries on their own.

• Vocal awareness: the pipeline now measures per-beat vocal activity (harmonic energy in the 200 to 4000 Hertz band, via harmonic-percussive separation), stores it in the analysis record, and penalizes any jump into or out of a strongly singing beat, scaled to each song's own typical distance; the walker cuts on the breath, not on the word.

• Sample-accurate web playback: the web page now schedules every beat on the Web Audio clock with a lookahead pump, instead of the drifting general-purpose timer; the per-beat gaps and smears are gone, and the chaos slider stays live.

• The analysis record format is now version 2 (with the per-beat vocal list); older caches re-analyze automatically on first use, per the versioned-record design in the Architecture.

• Measured on the owner's test song: jump-capable beats average 0.27 vocal activity against 0.34 for all beats - the graph's edges have already moved away from the singing.

• The full test suite (8 tests) stays green.

Iteration 39 (2026-08-09): Intelligent sentences - grouped phrases that refuse to be cut - by owner request.

• The analysis engine now detects SENTENCES: groups of adjacent 8-beat phrases that belong together. A phrase boundary survives as a sentence boundary only when the music genuinely changes there (the phrase sound-profiles differ) AND no vocal line bridges across the cut; a boundary is forced after four phrases so sentences cannot grow without limit. The detector scores every boundary as standardized-change minus a vocal-bridge penalty, keeping boundaries above the median.

• The jump gate upgraded from fixed phrases to detected sentences: with the (relabeled) "Jump on sentences (grouped phrases), not single beats" box checked, the walk may only jump as a SENTENCE ends - so a two-phrase vocal thought is never chopped between its phrases. Older records fall back to plain 8-beat gating.

• The visualizer shows the intelligence: taller gold ticks mark sentence starts (above the small orange phrase ticks), and the highlighted arcs are now sentence-to-sentence jumps; a Sentences metric card joined Phrases in all four rectangles.

REFINEMENT REFLECTION

The wickedest problem so far is that "sounds seamless" is perceptual: the similarity threshold was set by ear on rendered walks, and the honest fix is the learned-embeddings refinement, which makes the human ear the training signal instead of a hand-tuned constant.

Iteration 2 taught a small interface lesson: a probability control without a visible number invites guessing; showing the value costs three lines and removes the guess.
