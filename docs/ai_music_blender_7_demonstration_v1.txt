THE QUESTION THIS PHASE ANSWERS

"So, what can it do?"


THE CHOSEN DEMONSTRATION: A MINIMUM VIABLE PRODUCT ANYONE CAN RUN

The demonstration is the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) run, end to end, on the viewer's own song, on the viewer's own machine, with no network and no account:

  Step 1: bin/ai-music-blender analyze  YOUR_SONG.mp3
          - watch the self-hosted pipeline report tempo, key, beat count, and jump-edge count.

  Step 2: bin/ai-music-blender jukebox  YOUR_SONG.mp3 --render forever.wav --length 120
          - render two minutes of a song that never ends, then listen to forever.wav.

  Step 3: bin/ai-music-blender jukebox  YOUR_SONG.mp3 --seed 42
          - hear a live infinite walk; run it twice and hear the SAME walk, proving the shareable-seed promise.

  Step 4: bin/ai-music-blender mashup   SONG_A.mp3 SONG_B.mp3 -o blend.wav
          - watch the compatibility report (stretch and key shift applied), then listen to the blend.

  Step 5: bin/ai-music-blender jukebox  blend.wav
          - the crown jewel: the mash-up itself plays forever. Infinite mash-up radio.

  Step 6: bin/ai-music-blender web
          - open http://localhost:8765 and watch the classic arc diagram light up as the walk jumps.


WHAT THE DEMONSTRATION PROVES

• The promise of the Definition is kept: the analysis that killed every ancestor now runs locally, so this jukebox cannot be killed from outside.

• The Golden Rule holds: the classic infinite mode works, looks, and behaves as the beloved original did.

• The extension is real: two songs blend, and the blend feeds back into the infinite engine.


WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE (honest limits)

• It does not prove the learned models yet; version 1 similarity and compatibility are hand-tuned rules, and the learned upgrades are the planned refinements.

• Mash-up quality varies with the songs chosen; a compatibility score is printed so the viewer can see WHY a pairing worked or fought.

• The demonstration is reproducible by design: same song, same seed, same walk - a sceptic can run everything again.


DEMONSTRATION REFLECTION

The MVP shape was chosen over a benchmark because there is no respected public benchmark for "sounds seamless forever"; the honest demonstration is the viewer's own ears on the viewer's own music.

Laying this document beside ai_music_blender_1_definition_v1.txt: the promise was a self-hosted, unkillable, extendable infinite jukebox, and the demonstration shows exactly that.
