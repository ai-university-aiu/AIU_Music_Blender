AI MUSIC BLENDER - DSPARCD PHASE 5 - REFINEMENT - VERSION 1

Date: 2026-08-07


HOW REFINEMENT WORKS ON THIS PROJECT

Build in small tested steps; every improvement is recorded here, and every recorded improvement names the test that proves it.

The Golden Rule is enforced as a regression: the classic-mode tests (T1 through T4 of the Specification) must stay green after every change, forever.


ITERATION LOG

Iteration 1 (2026-08-07): First working build.

• The analysis pipeline, beat graph, jukebox walk, renderer, mash-up engine, and all three faces were built to Specification version 1, with the test suite of scenarios T1 through T7.

• Decision refined during building: per-beat features average the frames BETWEEN beats (not at the beat instant), because a beat is a span of sound, not a point; this noticeably improves jump seamlessness on synthetic tests.

• Decision refined during building: jump edges exclude the three nearest neighbor beats on each side, because trivially-adjacent jumps are inaudible non-events that crowd out interesting edges.

• Decision refined during building: the walk's end-of-song fallback jumps to the BEST-MATCHING earlier beat rather than beat zero, so the loop point is musical rather than mechanical.


PLANNED REFINEMENTS (the resume attacks, in order)

• Learned beat embeddings: replace the hand-weighted MFCC-plus-chroma distance with a small contrastive model; the analysis-record interface does not change (the Architecture planned for this).

• Reinforcement Learning walk policy: reward smooth transitions and coverage, penalize stuck loops; replaces the inside of the walker only.

• Natural-language steering: map "keep it mellow" to chaos, energy bias, and section weights.

• Mashability model: learn compatibility scoring from rendered examples, replacing the rule-based tempo-and-Camelot score.

• Generative patching (stretch): synthesize short transitions where no good jump exists.


REFINEMENT REFLECTION

The wickedest problem so far is that "sounds seamless" is perceptual: the similarity threshold was set by ear on rendered walks, and the honest fix is the learned-embeddings refinement, which makes the human ear the training signal instead of a hand-tuned constant.
